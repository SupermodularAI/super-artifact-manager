Approval rules (v4)

How expense requests move from the person who spent the money to the person who approves it, and when a payout happens.

Overview

1 Overview

This spec describes how expense requests move from the person who spent the money to the person who approves it, and when a payout happens. It covers single approvals, team limits and what the requester sees at each stage.

1.1 Goals

Approvers should clear their queue in minutes, not days. Requesters should always know where their request is and why.

1.2 Out of scope

Corporate cards, mileage and per-diem allowances are handled by separate flows and are not covered here.

2 Approval limits

Every request goes to the requester's manager. Requests above the team limit need a second approval from finance.

AmountApproversTarget time
Up to 750 €Manager1 working day
Above 750 €Manager, then finance3 working days

2.1 Team limits

Team leads can raise their own limit up to 2,000 € in settings; finance sees every change.

2.2 Out-of-office

When the manager is away, requests go to the person they named as backup. With no backup named, they go to finance.

Payouts

3 Payouts

An approved request is paid out in the next payout run. Runs happen every weekday at 14:00.

Request→Manager→Finance→Payout run→Bank transfer

3.1 Declined payouts

If the bank declines a transfer, the request goes back to Approved, not paid and the requester is told why.

We retry twice, 24 hours apart, before telling the requester.
