Expense policy

Expenses · Acme · Policy · Approved by finance

This policy says what the company pays for, how to claim it and how fast you get paid. It applies to everyone at Acme, including contractors on a monthly contract.

1 What we pay for

Travel for work, meals while travelling, hardware your manager approved and training that is part of your plan. If you are not sure, ask before you spend.

1.1 Travel

Book the cheapest reasonable option. Trains over planes for trips under four hours. Economy on flights under six hours.

1.2 Meals

Up to 35 € a day while travelling, receipts for anything above 10 €.

2 How to claim

Take a photo of the receipt in the app within 30 days. Pick a category, add a line about what it was for and send it to your manager.

2.1 Missing receipts

Write a short note saying what you bought, where and when. Missing receipts above 100 € need finance's approval.

3 Getting paid

Approved claims are paid in the next payout run, every weekday at 14:00. You see the status of every claim in the app.
